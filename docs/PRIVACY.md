# Privacy policy: AutoPocket Diary

**Effective date:** 30 October 2026
**Developer:** Mazid Ul Haque
**Applies to:** AutoPocket Diary (Google Play), AutoPocket Diary and AutoPocket Diary Auto (GitHub downloads)

## The short version

AutoPocket Diary is a personal money diary that works **entirely on your phone**. It has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything to the developer or anyone else. The developer collects **no data at all**.

## What the app reads

- **SMS messages (Google Play and Auto editions, only if you allow it).** The app reads the SMS in your inbox to find transaction messages from banks and mobile wallets, such as "Tk 1,200 debited from A/C ••1234". It uses them to record your income and spending automatically.
  - Messages that are not bank or wallet transactions, such as personal messages, OTPs and offers, are skipped and **never stored**.
  - For transaction messages, the app stores only what it needs: amount, date, direction, account or card last 4 digits, balance, merchant, and the message text so you can check an entry.
  - All reading happens **on your phone**. Nothing is uploaded.
  - You can turn the permission off at any time in Android settings. The app keeps working with SMS you share or paste in.
- **SMS you share, paste or import yourself.** These are handled the same way.
- **Entries you type by hand**, account names, budgets and settings.

## What the app does NOT do

- No internet access, so there are no servers, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
- No ads and no advertising ID.
- No account, sign-up, email or phone number.
- No access to contacts, location, camera or files beyond the ones you pick.
- The app never sends SMS, makes calls or moves money.

## How your data is stored

- Everything is kept in a database on your phone that is **encrypted**, with a key protected by the Android Keystore.
- The data is **excluded from Google/Android cloud backup**.
- The optional hidden zone is locked with a PIN, fingerprint or phone screen lock. Android checks your fingerprint; the app never sees it.

## Backups and exports you make

You can save a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV) export. The backup file is locked. The CSV export is **not** locked, so keep it private. These files go only where **you** save or share them, such as your phone storage or an app you pick. The developer never receives them.

## Sharing with third parties

None. The developer has no way to receive your data, so it is never sold, shared or transferred.

## Deleting your data

- Delete any entry inside the app, or erase the hidden zone.
- To remove everything at once: Android **Settings → Apps → AutoPocket Diary → Storage → Clear storage**.
- Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

## Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app has no internet access, changes cannot alter what the installed app can do with your data.
